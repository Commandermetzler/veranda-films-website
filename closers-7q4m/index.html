06 · Straight answers

Questions people ask.

Is there really no base pay?

Yes, this role is commission only. In return the rate is 20%, there is no cap, and you keep earning on reorders for six months.

How do I get paid?

On the 5th of each month for all orders paid in the previous month, by PayPal or Payoneer. If you were hired through a freelance platform, we pay through that platform.

When is a deal mine?

Every lead you work is assigned to you in our CRM, with your notes and call history. When a client from one of your leads pays, now or later by email, the order is yours. You get a monthly statement of your clients and commissions.

What leads do I get?

Researched lists of US vacation rental businesses with a direct business phone number, the local time and notes from our research (for example: their website is outdated or has no online booking). You call from a US number in a browser dialer. No autodialer, no recorded messages.

Are the films AI?

Yes, and we say so if asked. The camera movement is added to the owner's real photos, every clip is checked against the original, and nothing is invented. The customer approves the film before it's used.

Do I need to know video or web design?

No. You sell, we produce. The playbook gives you the answers to every common question.
