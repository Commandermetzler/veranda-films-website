06 · Straight answers

Questions people ask.

Is there really no base pay?

Yes, this role is commission only. In return the rate starts at 20% and rises to 25% from $5,000 and 30% from $10,000 of paid revenue in a month, there is no cap, and you keep earning on reorders for six months.

How long have you been doing this?

Straight answer: our phone team started in November 2026, so you would be one of our first closers. That is why the lead lists are fresh and why the first closers who deliver get the best leads and the higher rates first.

Who closes the deal?

You do, all the way: first call, Zoom with the demo website, payment link. You never build or deliver anything. Once the client pays, our system produces the website or film and our team handles support.

How do I get paid?

You work as an independent contractor, commission only. On the 5th of each month for all orders paid in the previous month, by PayPal or Payoneer. If you were hired through a freelance platform, we pay through that platform.

When is a deal mine?

Every lead you work is assigned to you in our CRM, with your notes and call history. When a client from one of your leads pays, now or later by email, the order is yours. You get a monthly statement of your clients and commissions.

What leads do I get?

Researched lists of vacation rental businesses and local businesses in the USA and Australia with a direct business phone number, the local time and notes from our research (for example: their website is outdated or has no online booking). You call from a local number in a browser dialer. No autodialer, no recorded messages.

Are the films AI?

Yes, and we say so if asked. The camera movement is added to the owner's real photos, every clip is checked against the original, and nothing is invented. The customer approves the film before it's used.

Do I need to know video or web design?

No. You sell, we produce. The playbook gives you the answers to every common question.
