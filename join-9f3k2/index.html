02 · Why the phone

Email gets us in the door. The phone closes.

We send thousands of emails a month to villa managers. The reply rate is normal for cold email, which means low. The managers who do reply are interested, but most of them never take the next step. On the phone, a real person can answer the one question that stops them, in thirty seconds. That's your job: turn "sounds interesting" into "here's my listing link".

What you sell

One sentence

"You have 187 villas. Nobody sends a film crew to 187 villas. We make all 187 from the photos you already have, and the first one you only pay for after you've approved it."

What a win looks like

A listing link

Not a nice chat. A manager sends us the link or the photos of one property so we can make the first film. That is the number we count, every single day.

06 · The first 30 days

One month to find out if this works, for both of us.

We start with a paid 30-day trial on a freelance basis. Clear targets from day one, and on day 30 we decide together whether we continue. If it works, you keep going, with the first results already in your pocket. If it doesn't, we part ways with no hard feelings.

Days 1–3 · Onboarding

Product and example films. Then the script, objections, role plays. Day 3: your first real calls.

Week 1 · Ramp-up

You build towards 120 dials a day. Short daily check-in on WhatsApp: what worked, what didn't, what we change in the script.

Weeks 2–4 · Full speed

Minimum 120 dials a day, target 150–200. We watch one number above all: listing links received. First commissions are paid out for every order that came in.

Day 30 · Decision

We look at the numbers together. Continue, adjust, or stop. If it's working, the next step is more leads, higher commission tiers, and later a team of callers you could lead.

07 · Questions people ask

Straight answers.

Is this MLM, or do I have to pay for anything?

No. You never pay us a single rupiah, not for training, not for software, not for leads. We pay you.

Who is behind Veranda Films?

Two founders from Germany, late twenties, who have lived in Bali for years and love the island. A small team based in Badung, Bali. We build the films, you bring the customers.

Do I need a licence? Is this a property job?

No licence. You don't sell, rent or broker property. You sell a marketing video to businesses in the United States.

Is the commission really uncapped?

Yes. 10% of every paid order, no ceiling. The more you close, the more you earn.

What if a customer doesn't pay?

Then there's no commission on that order. Your base salary is not affected.

Can I work from outside Bali?

Yes, from anywhere in Indonesia. Internet and a quiet room matter, your city doesn't.

What happens after the 30 days?

If the numbers are good, we continue and grow: more leads, better commission tiers, and the chance to lead a small team of callers later.
