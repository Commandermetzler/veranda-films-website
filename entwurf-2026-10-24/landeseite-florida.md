## Section 3 — How it works

**H2**
Three steps. You stay in your office.

**1. You send what you already have**
A listing link, or ten to fifteen of the original, full-size photos of one property. Originals, not the small versions from the listing page — the bigger the file, the sharper the film.

**2. We build the film**
Each photo becomes a slow, steady camera move. We cut about a dozen of them into one thirty-second film, add music, and check every single clip against the photo it came from. Anything that drifted from the original gets cut and rebuilt.

**3. You approve, then it is yours**
You see the film before anyone else does. Two rounds of changes are included. The film is yours to use everywhere, with no time limit — website, listings, ads, social media.

**Timing**
In about a week from the day your photos arrive.

## Section 8 — Questions people actually ask

**Is this AI?**
Yes. AI adds the camera movement to your own photos. We check every clip against the original photo, cut anything that isn't in it, and you approve the film before anyone sees it. It is also why we can deliver in about a week instead of a month. We never present it as drone footage.

**Do you come to the property?**
No. Nobody visits, nobody disturbs a guest, nobody needs a key or a drone permit. That is the whole point.

**What photos do you need?**
Ten to fifteen originals of one property — the full-size files from your photographer, not the compressed versions on the listing page. Exterior, pool or water, living area, kitchen, one or two bedrooms, bathroom, and the view. If you are unsure, send the folder and we will tell you what is missing.

**Who owns the film?**
You do. The film is yours to use everywhere, with no time limit — website, listings, ads, social media. The rights in your photos stay with you.

**How long does it take?**
In about a week from the day the photos arrive. Larger orders go out in batches on a schedule we agree before we start.

**Can you do all of our properties?**
Yes, and that is the part we are best at. We make the first one, you approve it, then we run the rest in batches with a per-property rate that drops with volume.

**How do we pay?**
Your first film is invoiced after you approve it. Everything after that is paid before we start. We invoice from our company and send bank details with the invoice.

**Do you only work in Florida?**
No — we work with managers anywhere. This page exists because Florida managers ask the same three questions, and we would rather answer them here than in a long email.
