Our pasties

A proper pasty, the Moonta way.

Visitors drive hours for it. Locals grew up on it. Every pasty is filled by hand out the back and comes out of the oven golden, with your choice of tomato sauce, our homemade sauce or chutney.

Eat in under the Cornish decor, or take a few home. Plenty of guests take a box of cold ones for the road.
