2. How to order

  1. Request. Send us a message through the contact form, by email or on WhatsApp with the property and the service you want.
  2. Written offer. We confirm the service, price in IDR and delivery date in writing, together with our client terms.
  3. Photos. You send the original photos of the property (at least 10, ideally 2,500 px wide or larger).
  4. Delivery. We deliver the finished files by download link, usually within a week. Two rounds of changes are included.
  5. Payment. You pay through a secure online payment link (see below).

3. Payment

We send a secure payment link for each order. You can pay by credit or debit card (Visa, Mastercard) or by bank transfer. The amount is charged in IDR; your bank converts it to your currency.

  • First film: paid only after you have seen and approved it. If you do not approve it, you pay nothing.
  • All further orders: paid in full in advance, before production starts.
  • Refunds: if we cannot deliver an ordered and prepaid service, we refund the full amount to the original payment method.

Our services are offered to businesses (B2B), mainly holiday-rental managers and property owners outside Indonesia.
