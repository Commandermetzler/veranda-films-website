The four steps

  1. Send one link. Reply “yes” to our email with the link to one property on your own website — or send 12+ original photos. We use only photos from your own website or photos you send us, never from booking platforms.
  2. We build the film. In about a week you get a 30-second film in three formats (16:9, 9:16, 4:5), with our watermark on it.
  3. You approve it. Two rounds of changes are included. If you want to use the film, we invoice €150 and send the clean files without watermark. If you don't, you owe nothing.
  4. The rest of your portfolio. Three films are €399. For more properties we quote one per-property rate in writing, within one business day — it drops with volume.

What it is, honestly

The camera movement is made with AI from your own photos. We check every clip against the original photo and cut anything that isn't really there. Nothing is filmed on site, no drone, no crew. The film is yours to use everywhere, with no time limit — your website, your ads, your social channels, your emails to past guests and your owner pitches.

Our example films so far are from Bali — that is where we started and where the first permissions came from. The method only needs your photos; the location does not matter. Watch the pilot films, then see it on your own property.

Quick answers

Do I need to send anything?

Only the link to one property on your own website. If your website has no gallery or only small photos, send 12 or more original photos instead (2,500 px wide or larger works best). Nobody needs to visit the property.

Who owns the photos?

You do, or your photographer. Sending them to us changes nothing about that; we use them only to make your film. Please send only photos you own or are allowed to use.

What if I don’t like the film?

Two rounds of changes are included. If you still don’t want to use it, you pay nothing and the watermarked preview is not used. If your photos cannot carry a film, we tell you before we start.

How long does it take?

About a week from the day we have your link or photos. Larger portfolios are delivered in batches on a schedule we agree upfront.

What does it cost after the first film?

€150 per film, or €399 for three. For more properties we quote one per-property rate in writing. Every film after the first one is paid up front.
